# How Many Cycles?

**Your phone already knows how many times its battery has gone around. This app shows you the number.**

Every battery is built to live for only so many full charges — its *cycle life* — and every phone maker picks a different number. Most never tell you yours. **How Many Cycles?** reads the cycle count your phone itself reports (Android 14+) and shows it big and plain, along with your phone model, current battery level, and charging state.

## The rated life

If your phone's maker ever published a cycle rating for your model (cycles until the battery keeps about 80% of its health), the app shows it, with a simple bar of how much of that rated life you've used.

The figures come only from manufacturer-published ratings and the EU's standardized energy-label database — never estimates. Where a maker's marketing number and the EU label disagree, the app uses the label.

And when a maker never published a rating at all — as with the entire US Moto G line — the app says so, in plain words:

> **No published rating: the maker never released a battery cycle-life rating for this model.**

## Privacy

- **No internet permission.** The app cannot phone home.
- No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
- Nothing leaves your phone, because nothing can.

## Install

The install file lives in this repository as **HowManyCycles.apk**. Download it, open it, and allow the install — Android may ask permission because the app doesn't come from the Play Store. That's normal.
